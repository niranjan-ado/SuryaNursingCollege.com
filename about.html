About the college

The establishment, objectives and leadership of Surya Nursing Educational College.

About the college

Surya Nursing Educational College was established in 2024. The institution operates with the dual objective of providing structured nursing education and contributing to regional manpower development in the healthcare sector.

The college offers comprehensive educational programs including Auxiliary Nurse & Midwife (ANM), General Nursing Midwifery (GNM), and a Basic B.Sc. in Nursing. The campus infrastructure is equipped with lecture halls, an auditorium, and specialized laboratories that strictly comply with the operational norms mandated by the Indian Nursing Council.

Mission, vision and objectives

Institutional goal

To contribute to the socio-economic and ethical development of the nation by providing high-quality education through dedicated faculty and state-of-the-art infrastructure.

Our mission

To bring quality healthcare to India, closer to individuals in need. The institution strives to define excellence in healthcare and set verifiable benchmarks across the medical value chain.

Our vision

Looking ahead, the college is dedicated to helping build a brighter and healthier future for citizens, working to position the nation as a preferred global healthcare destination.

Quality education, all-round development and character building.

Dr. Vijay Kumar

Chairman & Director

About this website

Designed and built by Adostrophe. The colours come from the college crest: the sun by day, deep indigo by night, after the motto अहर्निशं सेवामहे.

How the site was built
  • Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, page builder or build step.
  • CSS in cascade layers, with one set of colour tokens using light-dark() for the day and night themes.
  • Native ES modules; code for tabs, the photo viewer and the hero film loads only on pages that use them.
  • Self-hosted, subset typefaces from Ek Type: Anek and Mukta, with Anek Devanagari for the motto.
  • One SVG icon sprite, WebP images in two sizes, and a drone loop that plays only when data and motion settings allow.
  • Strict Content Security Policy with no inline scripts or styles; structured data for search engines.
  • Checked with automated browser tests, the HTML validator and axe accessibility audits in both themes.
  • Hosted on Azure Static Web Apps, deployed from GitHub. More about the build.
