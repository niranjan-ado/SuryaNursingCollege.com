How to apply

Your step-by-step guide to joining Surya Nursing Educational College.

The application process

Our admission process is transparent and based on merit, as per the guidelines of the state council.

  1. Check eligibility

    Ensure you meet the age and educational requirements. Review the courses.

  2. Entrance test

    Admissions are strictly based on qualifying the J.C.E.C.E.B. entrance test. See the exam schedule.

  3. Counseling

    Qualified candidates will be called for counseling and seat allotment.

  4. Final admission

    Visit our campus with the required documents to complete admission formalities.

Documents to bring

Please prepare the following original documents along with photocopies for verification during final admission.

  • J.C.E.C.E.B. admit card and rank card
  • 10th and 12th mark sheets
  • College or school leaving certificate
  • Character certificate
  • Domicile certificate
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • 8 passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar card

Admissions questions

When do admissions for the next session start?

The admission schedule, including application form deadlines, is determined and announced by the J.C.E.C.E.B. We recommend checking their official website regularly for the latest notifications.

Is there a direct admission or management quota?

No, all admissions are strictly conducted through the centralized counseling process based on the J.C.E.C.E.B. entrance exam rankings to ensure fairness and transparency.

What is the fee structure for the courses?

For the most current and detailed information on our fee structure, including tuition, hostel, and other charges, please contact our admissions office directly. They will provide you with a comprehensive breakdown.

Are there any scholarships available?

Students may be eligible for various state and national scholarship programs. Our administrative office can provide guidance on the application process for these schemes once your admission is confirmed.

More questions and answers

Have more questions?

Our admissions team is ready to assist you with any queries about the process, our campus, or student life.
