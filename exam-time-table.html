Pre-admission entrance exam dates

All applications and examinations are conducted through the official Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (J.C.E.C.E.B.). Please adhere to the following deadlines.

CourseOnline application deadlineEntrance exam date
Auxiliary Nurses & Midwife (ANM)10 July 202627 July 2026
General Nurses & Midwife (GNM)10 July 202627 July 2026
B.Sc. Nursing11 July 202626 July 2026
Always confirm on the official website. J.C.E.C.E.B. publishes the dates and any changes for each session.

Visit jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in
