Approvals and affiliation

  • Indian Nursing Council (INC)

    Recognized and approved by the statutory body of the Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi, under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

  • Jharkhand Nurses Registration Council

    Approved by the Jharkhand Nurses Registration Council (JNRC), Ranchi, for state-level clinical practice and registration.

  • Sido Kanhu Murmu University

    Affiliated with Sido Kanhu Murmu University (SKMU), Dumka, serving as the degree-awarding university for collegiate programs.

Nursing courses

The college offers three nursing programs, from diploma certificates to an undergraduate degree. Admission is through the J.C.E.C.E.B. entrance test.

  • 2years

    ANM (Auxiliary Nurse & Midwife)

    Diploma program focusing on primary healthcare and midwifery.

  • 3years

    GNM (General Nursing Midwifery)

    Diploma program emphasizing general healthcare, maternity, and clinical nursing.

  • 4years

    B.Sc Nursing (Basic)

    Undergraduate degree providing advanced clinical training and theoretical foundations.

About the institution

Surya Nursing Educational College was established in 2024 with the primary objective of providing high-quality nursing education and developing skilled healthcare professionals. The institution focuses on the holistic preparation of nurses through rigorous academic and clinical training.

Our goal is to contribute to the socio-economic and ethical development of the nation. We achieve this by cultivating competent, liberal-minded healthcare professionals through dedicated faculty and state-of-the-art infrastructure capable of meeting the highest standards of medical practice.

Quality education, all-round development and character building.

A safe campus to live and learn

Surya Nursing Educational College is spread over 5.5 acres, with an administrative block, academic block, classrooms, labs, library, computer lab, auditorium and hostel.
